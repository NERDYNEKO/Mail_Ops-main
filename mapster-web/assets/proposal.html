EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
WHO WE ARE

One app that guides every visitor

Mapster is an indoor navigation and exhibition engagement platform: a single, installable web app that guides visitors turn by turn to any stall, hall or facility, while giving organizers real visibility into how their venue is actually being explored.

We built Mapster to solve a problem every large exhibition faces. Visitors spend more time searching for stalls than engaging with them, exhibitors in less obvious corners get overlooked, and organizers have no real data on visitor movement to show sponsors or plan the next event around.

THE CHALLENGE

What exhibitions struggle with today

1

Visitors get lost

Large venues with dozens or hundreds of stalls overwhelm visitors, with no digital way to search for a name, category or hall and be guided there.

2

Low booth engagement

Exhibitors away from the main entrance or a busy walkway are never found by a large share of visitors, which hurts their ROI and your renewals.

3

No visitor insight

Organizers rarely know which halls, stalls or categories visitors actually explored, data that matters to sponsors and to planning your next event.

4

One way information

Printed maps and static signage cannot be updated live for a schedule change, a moved stall or a time sensitive announcement.

OUR SOLUTION
WHAT YOU GET

What Mapster brings to your venue

One installable web app, with no app store download required, covering the whole visitor journey, plus a dashboard that gives you the visibility to run the event with confidence.

Real time indoor navigation

Turn by turn walking directions to any stall, hall, washroom or facility, accurate indoors where GPS alone fails.

Searchable exhibition map

A live, categorized directory of every exhibitor and stall. Visitors search by name or category and get routed instantly.

Treasure hunt and gamification

Points, streaks and a leaderboard reward visitors for actually visiting stalls, turning footfall into a game that benefits every exhibitor, not just the ones near the entrance.

Announcements and guidebook

Push notifications and an in app guidebook keep visitors current on schedule changes, offers and event info in real time.

Multi language support

The app already speaks 7 Indian languages, so language is never a barrier to visitors finding their way.

Admin dashboard and analytics

Your team gets a live view of visitor activity, stall verifications and engagement, real data you can bring back to sponsors and exhibitors.
